SKIN CARE

Acne Treatment

Acne can cause pimples, blackheads, whiteheads and inflamed spots. It may affect the face, chest or back and can sometimes leave marks.

Common concerns

  • Pimples and clogged pores
  • Redness and inflamed acne
  • Acne marks and uneven skin texture

How we can help

A dermatologist can assess your skin type, acne severity and possible contributing factors before recommending suitable skin care, medication or other appropriate treatment options.

HAIR & SCALP CARE

Hair Loss Treatment

Hair fall and thinning can have different causes, including hereditary factors, stress, nutritional issues and certain scalp conditions.

Common concerns

  • Increased hair shedding
  • Gradual hair thinning
  • Itching, flaking or scalp discomfort

How we can help

A consultation can help identify possible reasons for hair loss. Depending on the findings, a dermatologist may recommend scalp care, appropriate treatment or further investigations.

SKIN HEALTH

Skin Allergy Treatment

Skin irritation may appear as itching, redness, swelling or rashes. Triggers can include certain products, environmental exposures or underlying skin conditions.

Common concerns

  • Itchy or irritated skin
  • Redness and skin rashes
  • Reactions to skin care products

How we can help

A dermatologist can review your symptoms and possible triggers, assess the affected skin and suggest suitable management. Treatment depends on the cause of the reaction.

Book a Consultation
FACIAL SKIN CARE

Face Care

Facial skin can be affected by dryness, pigmentation, uneven tone, sensitivity and changes in skin texture. Choosing appropriate care starts with understanding your skin's needs.

Common concerns

  • Uneven skin tone and pigmentation
  • Dryness and skin texture concerns
  • Facial skin care and maintenance

How we can help

Depending on your concerns and skin type, a dermatologist can discuss suitable skin care routines and appropriate clinical or cosmetic treatment options.

NEED TO KNOW MORE?

Frequently Asked Questions

Some useful information before your first visit.

How can I book an appointment?

Click any Book an Appointment button. You can register as a patient and use the appointment booking system to request a visit.

Which skin and hair concerns can I discuss?

You can discuss concerns such as acne, hair loss, scalp problems, skin irritation, rashes, pigmentation and other skin care questions. The dermatologist can assess whether your concern falls within the clinic's services.

Will every patient receive the same treatment?

No. Appropriate care depends on the patient's symptoms, skin type, medical history and assessment. Your dermatologist can discuss suitable options for your individual needs.

What should I bring to my consultation?

If available, bring details of your current skin care products, previous treatments, medications and any relevant medical records. These can help your dermatologist understand your concerns.
